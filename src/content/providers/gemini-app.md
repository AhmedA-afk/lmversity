## What this page covers

**Gemini the app** — gemini.google.com and the mobile apps, as distinct from the [Gemini API/Vertex](/providers/google): Deep Research, Workspace integration, Gems, and Google's ecosystem leverage. Verified against Google's support docs on 2026-09-15.

## Product map

- **Core chat** — the Gemini app across web/mobile; model tiers map to Google's plan structure (free tier vs Google One AI plans vs Workspace accounts).
- **Deep Research** — Google's agentic research mode: browses, synthesizes, and produces a sourced report — the counterpart to ChatGPT's Deep Research and Claude's Research.
- **Multimodal** — the genuine strength: image/video/audio input and generation (including native image generation and Veo-class video on supported tiers), grounded in Google's multimodal stack.
- **Workspace integration** — Gemini inside Gmail/Docs/Sheets/Meet/Drive for Workspace accounts: summarize threads, draft in Docs, analyze in Sheets — the distribution nobody else has.
- **Gems** — custom assistants with their own instructions (the GPTs analog), shareable.
- **Extensions** — YouTube, Maps, Flights, Hotels, Workspace data pulled into chats.
- **NotebookLM pairing** — Google's source-bounded product is a sibling, not a feature: see [/providers/notebooklm](/providers/notebooklm).

## The selection read

Gemini's edge is **ecosystem**: if your work lives in Google Workspace, Gemini-in-Docs/Gmail/Meet is leverage no standalone chat app replicates — plus the strongest native multimodal breadth. Its gaps: Workspace features require the Workspace purchase (the app's best features are gated behind the ecosystem), and the chat-app UX itself isn't the differentiator — the integrations are.

## When to choose it

**Choose Gemini when** Google Workspace is your work substrate (the integration value is real, not marketing), when multimodal work (image/video/audio in and out) is core, or when Deep Research plus Google's knowledge stack fits your research workflow.

**When not to choose it.** Outside the Google ecosystem it loses its edge — a strong but not uniquely-strong chat app. Data/privacy review should happen at the Workspace-account level (admin-controlled) vs consumer-account level — different data terms.

**Migration considerations.** Gems and Workspace integrations are Google-specific; chat exports exist. The cross-product selection framework lives at [/answers/how-to-choose-an-ai-chat-product](/answers/how-to-choose-an-ai-chat-product).
