## What this page covers

**Claude the app** — claude.ai across web/desktop/mobile, as distinct from the [Anthropic API](/providers/anthropic): research, artifacts, projects, and the extended-work surfaces. Verified against Anthropic support docs on 2026-09-15.

## Product map

- **Core chat** — Claude's conversational surface with model selection across the current lineup and **extended thinking** (visible reasoning effort on supported models) as a first-class control.
- **Research** — Claude's agentic research mode: multi-source investigation with citations, integrating web and workspace sources.
- **Artifacts** — the signature surface: generated documents, code, and interactive apps rendered beside the chat, editable and shareable — the "work product" half of the product.
- **Projects** — workspace containers bundling chats + uploaded knowledge + custom instructions around a body of work; project knowledge grounds every chat inside it.
- **Files and analysis** — document upload for grounded Q&A; analysis tooling for data work on supported plans.
- **Styles and memory** — response-style customization and preference memory across sessions.
- **Integrations** — connectors (Google Workspace and others) pulling work context into chats; the ecosystem-extension layer.
- **Plan tiers** — Free → Pro/Max → Team/Enterprise, controlling models, usage, and admin/data features.

## The selection read

Claude's differentiation as a work product is **work-artifact quality**: artifacts + projects + writing quality make it strong for document-and-code artifact workflows, and its long-document handling is the common reason users pick it. Its gaps: narrower consumer feature breadth than ChatGPT (no native image generation surface in the app), and — like every chat product — the data terms differ by tier.

## When to choose it

**Choose Claude when** the work is document/code-artifact-shaped (drafting, editing, code artifacts, long-document Q&A — artifacts+projects is a genuinely differentiated workflow), when extended-thinking visibility matters, or when Claude models are already your pick and you want the vendor's own app.

**When not to choose it.** Image generation, the broadest "everything app" breadth, or multi-model access — Claude is Anthropic-only. Source-bounded academic synthesis is NotebookLM's design point.

**Migration considerations.** Projects and artifacts are Claude-specific containers; exports exist but workflows don't port wholesale. See [/answers/how-to-choose-an-ai-chat-product](/answers/how-to-choose-an-ai-chat-product) for the cross-product decision.
