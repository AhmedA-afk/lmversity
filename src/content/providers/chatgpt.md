## What this page covers

**ChatGPT the product** — the app at chatgpt.com, as distinct from the [OpenAI API](/providers/openai): what the consumer/team surfaces actually do for research, files, analysis, and custom workflows. Verified 2026-09-15 (OpenAI's help center is bot-protected — HTTP 403 to automated fetch — so feature specifics carry this date).

## Product map

- **Core chat** — the general assistant surface across web, desktop, and mobile; model picker across OpenAI's current lineup; **memory** (saved + referenced-from-history) personalizes responses across sessions.
- **Deep Research** — the agentic research mode: multi-step browsing with a sourced report as output — the flagship "research product" capability.
- **Files and data analysis** — upload documents/spreadsheets; the analysis surface runs code over your data (the "Advanced Data Analysis" lineage) for charts, transforms, and Q&A.
- **Canvas** — the side-by-side writing/editing surface for longer documents.
- **Images** — generation and editing inside the chat surface.
- **Projects** — workspace containers grouping chats, files, and instructions around a body of work.
- **Custom GPTs** — packaged assistants with their own instructions/knowledge/actions; the "custom workflows" answer; shareable and (for builders) monetizable.
- **Voice and multimodal** — voice mode, image input, vision across the app.
- **Plan tiers** — Free → Plus/Pro → Team/Enterprise: the tier controls models, limits, and admin features (workspaces, SSO, data controls on business tiers).

## The selection read

ChatGPT's strengths as a work product: broadest feature breadth (research + files + images + voice + custom GPTs in one app) and the deepest "app around the model" investment. Its gaps for selection purposes: model lineup is OpenAI-only; data controls differ sharply by tier (consumer vs Team/Enterprise data-use terms are *different products* in policy terms — check yours).

## When to choose it

**Choose ChatGPT when** breadth-in-one-app is the requirement (research→analysis→drafting→images without switching tools), when custom GPTs solve a shareable-workflow need, or when your org already provisions it at a business tier with appropriate data terms.

**When not to choose it.** Source-bounded synthesis (NotebookLM is purpose-built), multi-model access (it's OpenAI-only), and workflows where the API is the right tool anyway — the app and API are different purchases with different data terms.

**Migration considerations.** Projects/custom GPTs are ChatGPT-specific containers; exports exist but the workflows themselves don't port. The selection framework at [/answers/how-to-choose-an-ai-chat-product](/answers/how-to-choose-an-ai-chat-product) covers the cross-product decision.
