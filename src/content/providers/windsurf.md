## What this page covers

**Windsurf** — the other AI-first IDE (Codeium's editor): Cascade agent workflow, project configuration, and the "flow" positioning that distinguishes it from Cursor's model-picker stance. Verified against Windsurf docs on 2026-09-15 — a volatile product (ownership and packaging have moved), so feature specifics carry the verification date.

## Product map

- **Cascade** — Windsurf's agent: the name for its agentic workflow — codebase-aware, multi-file editing, terminal-command execution, and the "flow-state" framing of agent+human working in one continuous loop.
- **Project configuration** — rules/memories and workspace configuration analogous to the category's repo-instruction conventions.
- **Previews and deployment** — in-editor app preview plus deploy integrations — a surface Cursor doesn't emphasize the same way.
- **Model selection** — multi-model support with Windsurf's own model lineup alongside third-party options.
- **Plugins/MCP** — the standard extensibility surface for tools and context.

## The positioning point

Windsurf vs Cursor is the category's direct rivalry: both are AI-first editor-forks, both do agentic editing. Windsurf's differentiation leans on Cascade's flow framing and its deploy/preview surface; Cursor's on the model table and rules ecosystem. Neither difference is durable enough to pick on marketing — the task-based comparison row exists precisely because this is a try-both decision.

## When to choose it

**Choose Windsurf when** Cascade's workflow clicks for your team (the honest answer is: try both Windsurf and Cursor on the same repo — see the comparison lab), when the preview/deploy surface matters to your app workflow, or when pricing/packaging favors it at your team size.

**When not to choose it.** Same caveat class as Cursor — adopting it means adopting an editor; terminal-first/CI automation needs a different tool. And given the product's volatility (ownership, packaging churn), pin your evaluation to the current docs rather than older reviews.

**Migration considerations.** Rules/MCP port as in the rest of the category; the editor and Cascade workflow are the product — evaluate on your actual repositories.
