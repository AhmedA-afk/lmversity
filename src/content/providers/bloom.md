## What this page covers

**BLOOM** — the BigScience workshop's 176B-parameter multilingual model (2022-era), covered here as historical context because of what it proved: a global volunteer research collaboration could train and release a frontier-scale open model — with the most transparent process of its era. Verified against the Hugging Face card on 2026-09-15.

## Why it still matters

- **The proof of concept for open science.** Before BLOOM, "big model" meant corporate labs. A thousand researchers across dozens of countries trained one openly — the template OLMo and every open-release program inherited.
- **Multilingual from the start.** 46 natural languages + 13 programming languages — multilingual-by-design years before it was a checklist feature.
- **Governance as artifact.** The BigScience governance process (ethical charters, working groups, documented decisions) is itself a case study in responsible model release.

## License

The **`bigscience-bloom-rail-1.0`** license — an early Responsible AI License (RAIL): permissive use with behavior restrictions attached. It's the historical ancestor of today's "permissive + conditions" license family — understanding RAIL explains the license middle-ground between Apache and fully-restricted.

## Where it fits today

Honest placement: **educational and historical**, not a deployment candidate. Model capability has moved generations ahead; BLOOM's value now is as the documented case study of open-model development — the training reports and governance docs are still the best public record of how a large model gets made. For actual Indic/multilingual work, the current families are the answer.
